FAQ

Questions before you export.

These answers help users understand exactly what Folio CV is for and how it fits into their application workflow.

Is Folio CV completely free?

Yes. Folio CV is free to use. You can create your resume, switch templates, customize colors, and export the final CV as a PDF.

Can I customize my resume design?

Yes. You can adjust templates, fonts, and colors for headings, body text, accent areas, sidebars, and backgrounds directly in the live preview.

Is the CV suitable for job applications and ATS systems?

The templates are clearly structured, optimized for A4 export, and built with a readable information hierarchy. That helps both recruiters and many ATS systems.

Do I need an account to create a CV?

No. You can start immediately, build your resume online, and download it as a PDF without creating an account.

Who is Folio CV for?

Folio CV works well for students, early-career professionals, freelancers, creatives, and experienced candidates who want a modern resume or CV.

Folio CV is free and open-source.
