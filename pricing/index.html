Which plan should someone pick?

The plans unlock the same Pro workspace. The difference is the billing rhythm. Weekly is for short bursts, monthly is for an active search, and yearly is for people who keep career documents current over time.

Weekly: quick sprint

Use weekly when the user needs to prepare a CV, cover letter, and a few applications quickly. It keeps the entry price low and fits short-term access.

Monthly: active search

Use monthly when the user is applying over several weeks, tailoring CVs for multiple roles, and tracking replies or interviews.

Yearly: ongoing career workspace

Use yearly when the user wants a permanent place for CV versions, profile updates, job search history, and recurring AI-assisted application work.

How payment and login work

The app should not collect card details itself. It sends the selected plan to secure checkout, the payment provider collects payment details, and the app receives the subscription status. Only then does the Pro workspace unlock.

This means the app can avoid asking for an email before pricing. The customer enters their details during checkout, and the app uses the completed checkout session to connect subscription status with account access.

Pricing questions

Does Basic need a credit card?

No. Basic is the free CV builder and PDF export workflow. It should remain available without payment or registration.

Can users log in without Pro?

The intended product rule is no. Users can build and export without login, but account access is part of Pro and depends on active subscription status.

What happens if Pro is canceled?

The subscription remains active until the paid period ends. After that, Pro features should lock again while Basic remains available.
